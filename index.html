These are editable example values, not confirmed details of your installation. Each phone remembers its own settings.

Important: This is an estimate. GivEnergy readings are used only if the live-data request succeeds; otherwise the figures remain manually editable. It assumes solar stays steady during the cycle, and compares using surplus solar now against saving that solar for later. It does not predict evening battery charge or account for changing household demand, variable appliance power, or battery charging/discharging limits.

Appliance estimates vary considerably by model, programme and temperature. Enter figures from your appliance if known. For dishwashers and washing machines, avoiding an extra cycle can matter more than timing.
